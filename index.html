Duo queue in League of Legends: who can queue with whom (2026 rules)

Adam Shanin · 25 November 2026

Playing ranked with a friend is one of the best parts of League of Legends, until the client refuses to let you queue together. Ranked Solo/Duo limits who can duo by rank, and the rules tighten at the top. Here is how they work in 2026, based on Riot Support's article on ranked tiers and queues.

Solo/Duo: one tier either way, up to Diamond

From Iron to Platinum, you can duo with anyone one tier above or below you, in any division. A Gold player can queue with Silver, Gold or Platinum friends; an Iron player with Iron or Bronze. From Emerald the windows narrow: Diamond IV to II can queue with Emerald II and above, while Diamond I cannot queue with Emerald at all.

Master and above

  • A Master player can duo with a Diamond I player only if the Master player has under 200 LP, or with another Master player within 400 LP.
  • Grandmaster and Challenger cannot duo in Solo/Duo.
  • On the Korean server, players cannot queue with Master or higher at all.

Hidden MMR also counts

The visible rank is not the only check. A player whose hidden matchmaking rating (MMR) is at Master level or above is treated as solo-only, and Emerald to Diamond II players cannot queue with partners whose MMR is at Master level. That is why two friends with compatible badges are sometimes still blocked: one of them is rated much higher than their badge shows. The LoL MMR checker estimates where your hidden rating sits from the LP you gain and lose, which explains most of these surprises.

Flex: more freedom, harder games

Ranked Flex has no rank restrictions for Diamond and below, and it allows parties of 1, 2, 3 or 5. Master and above can play Flex only with partners who are at least Emerald. Riot warns that big rank gaps in a Flex party produce harder matchups, because the system balances the lobby around the strongest player.

Does duo help you climb?

A good duo partner who plays a complementary role, such as jungle with mid or support with ADC, can raise your win rate through coordination. But the matchmaking compensates for the party, and a mismatched duo where one player is much stronger usually ends up with harder opponents for the weaker player. If you want to climb, duo with someone near your own level and role-pair deliberately.

Quick checks before you queue

  1. Are you within one tier of each other (Iron to Platinum)?
  2. Is either of you Diamond I or above? Check the narrower windows.
  3. Still blocked? One of you probably has a much higher hidden MMR; try Flex instead.

The LoL duo queue checker takes both ranks and tells you instantly whether Solo/Duo allows the pair and what Flex allows instead. And if your friends play on a different region, a level 30 League of Legends account on that server gets you into ranked with them without levelling from scratch.
